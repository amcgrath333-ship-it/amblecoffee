Good to know

Questions, answered.

How much notice do you need?

72 hours for most orders, and two weeks for the coffee bar. For last-minute requests, call us and we’ll always try.

Do you cater for dietary needs?

Yes. We offer gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan options, and we’ll label everything clearly.

Where do you deliver?

Across Chilliwack and the Fraser Valley. Delivery is free within 10 km of the café.

Is there a minimum order?

Delivery orders start at $90. Pickup orders have no minimum.
