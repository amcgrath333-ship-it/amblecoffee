Good to know

Before you wander over.

Are dogs welcome?

Always, on the patio. There’s a water bowl by the door and a biscuit jar behind the counter.

Can I work from the café?

Of course. There’s free wifi and plenty of outlets. On busy weekend mornings we ask laptops to share the big table.

Is there parking?

Free parking right outside, plus bike racks by the patio.

Is the café accessible?

Yes. Step-free entry, an accessible washroom and a baby-change table.

Do you have plant-based options?

Our oat and almond milks are made in house every morning, and there are always vegan bakes on the counter.
